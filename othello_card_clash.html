OTHELLO: CARD CLASH

How to play

  • Each turn: gain energy (ramps +1/turn, max 10), draw 1 card.
  • Play cards you can afford, then place one disc.
  • Discs flip enemy discs sandwiched in a line, standard Othello.
  • Shielded discs (gold ring) absorb one flip.
  • No valid move? Play cards, then Pass. Two passes in a row ends the game.
  • Most discs when the board fills (or both pass) wins.
